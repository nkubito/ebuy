Questions? We've got answers.

A few useful details about shopping on eBuy Rwanda. Some services are still being built, and we'll update this page as they become available.

What is eBuy Rwanda?

eBuy is a marketplace concept designed to help customers discover products from independent sellers in Rwanda.

Are the products currently live listings?

Not yet. The current storefront uses demonstration products. Live seller listings will be connected after seller onboarding and catalog tools are completed.

Can I place an order or pay online?

Online checkout and payment collection are not enabled yet. Please do not treat the demonstration cart as a confirmed order.

How does the shopping cart work?

You can add sample products, adjust quantities and remove items. The cart is saved in this browser only and does not submit an order.

How can I become a seller?

Seller onboarding is planned. The team will publish the application process when seller registration and approval are ready.

Which currency does eBuy use?

Prices in the current prototype are displayed in Rwandan francs (RWF).

Where does eBuy deliver?

Delivery coverage and fees have not yet been configured. Delivery information will be published before ordering becomes available.

How can I contact eBuy?

Customer support channels will be published as the marketplace moves toward launch.
